The WOFF2 files in this folder are redistributed under the
[SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).
They are subset to Latin, common punctuation, arrows and geometric shapes
and converted from the original TTFs with `fonttools`; no Reserved Font
Names are declared, so the family names are kept.
